Background

About Me

I'm an industrial engineer (POSTECH, NC State) with over a decade in manufacturing and distribution: warehouse layout and material flow, inventory and material planning, and inbound logistics.

I specialize in translating large, messy datasets into clear operational decisions, whether that's catching a shortage the plan can't see, fixing packaging data before it decides how many trucks you order, or showing a warehouse as a map instead of a list.

When a spreadsheet stops being enough, I build the tool. The demos above are rebuilt with fictional data so anyone can open them. I work in English and Korean.
