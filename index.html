About Me

I'm a supply chain and analytics professional with a background in Industrial Engineering (POSTECH, NC State) and over a decade of hands-on experience in demand planning, inventory optimization, and operational analytics.

I specialize in translating large, messy datasets into clear operational decisions — whether that's building statistical forecast models, writing complex SQL pipelines, or designing dashboards that actually get used.

Beyond the day job, I build tools: a SAP WMS inventory dashboard, a warehouse operations tracking app now in production use. I believe the best supply chain analysts are part engineer, part data scientist.
